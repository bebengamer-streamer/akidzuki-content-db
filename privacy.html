Privacy Policy

Akidzuki Flight Content DB is a desktop application for organizing, scheduling, and analyzing social media content.

The application may connect to TikTok and other supported platforms using their official authorization systems.

The application may access basic account information and public video information only when authorized by the user.

Akidzuki Flight Content DB does not collect or store TikTok passwords.

Authorization tokens may be stored locally on the user's computer to maintain authorized account connections.

User information is not sold to third parties.

Users may disconnect their account or revoke authorization at any time.
